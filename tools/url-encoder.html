📖 How to Use

1
Enter URL or Text

Type or paste the URL or text you want to encode. e.g. https://example.com/search?q=hello

2
Encode or Decode

Click 🔼 Encode to percent-encode special characters, or 🔽 Decode to revert them.

3
Review and Copy

Check the result and click 📋 Copy to save to your clipboard.

💡 Tip: URL encoding is essential when query parameters contain non-ASCII characters like Hangul or spaces.
